From registration to verification

Issued once. Owned by the student. Checked by any employer.

The same path is available in the portal. Registration, identity, university review, the AI check, the contract, the ledger entry, and the employer result are all live screens.

01

Register

A student, university, or employer opens an account. A wrong password is refused.

 02

Create a digital identity

A student receives an identity such as did:edu:110060461, tied to the qualifications issued to that person.

 03

Confirm the identity

A new student enters the code shown on the next screen. The prepared student account is already confirmed.

 04

Upload a certificate

The learner submits the qualification, institution, year, and document name. It stays pending until the issuer acts.

 05

University review

Authorised staff open the submission. The institution, not the platform, decides whether the award is real.

 06

AI fraud check

The check returns an anomaly score and the reasons behind it. A high-risk file cannot be issued until a person confirms the review.

 07

Smart contract

The contract checks the issuer, the learner identity, the credential type, and duplicate awards before it writes anything.

 08

Blockchain record

A transaction id and status are stored. The personal document stays off the public record.

 09

Token created

An EDU token, such as #EDU10245, is minted to the student who owns the credential.

 10

Share with a QR code

The student generates a code and a link for one credential.

 11

Employer verifies

The employer enters the ID or opens the link and sees Verified or Revoked.

A second path covers revocation. An authorised issuer marks the credential revoked, the contract writes a new status event, and the next employer check shows that status.
