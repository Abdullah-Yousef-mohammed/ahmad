Planning assumptions

Institutions and employers pay. The student wallet stays free.

These figures are for the pilot proposal. They are not published market prices. The basic wallet is free so students are not charged just to prove a credential is authentic.

Universities

Starter

RM1,000 / month

For a small training provider. Limited monthly issuance, a review desk, and status checks.

Universities

Professional

Custom

Higher issuance volume, dashboards, and a branded landing page for the institution.

Employers

Verification

RM500 / month

A team plan for repeated checks. Pay-per-check packs are available for smaller hiring teams.

Student wallet

Free. Optional premium extras, such as a longer portfolio and a verified CV export, can be added later. EduChain Verify does not sell student data.

Illustrative mix: 10 institutions at RM1,000 a month (RM120,000 a year), RM40,000 of issuance and setup, and 10 employers at RM500 a month (RM60,000). Planning total RM220,000 a year before operating costs.
