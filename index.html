The problem

Paper and loose PDF files are slow to trust.

  • 01Certificates are easy to alter once they leave the campus.
  • 02Universities answer the same verification request again and again.
  • 03Students resend the same documents to every recruiter.
  • 04Employers have no single, fast authenticity check.

Five technologies, one workflow

Each layer has a job.

Identity

Digital identity

The platform knows who is acting before anyone can issue or share a record.

AI

Fraud check

Document review flags tampering and explains the score. A person still decides.

Contract

Smart contract

Authorised rules record issuance and revocation instead of a one-off email.

Ledger

Blockchain record

A tamper-evident proof and status event are stored. The personal file is not put on a public chain.

Wallet

Digital ownership

The student holds the credential token and chooses when to share it.
